# Step 06: Microsoft Fabric

**Goal**: rebuild the pipeline on Fabric and understand what the platform manages for me.

## Theory (to expand)

- **Eventstream**: streaming ingestion (it also accepts Kafka producers).
- **Eventhouse and KQL**: a database for time-series data and its query language.
- **OneLake**: the single storage layer, with tables in Delta/Parquet format.
- **Hot and cold path**: Eventhouse for real time, OneLake for history.
- **dbt on Fabric**: `dbt-fabric` (Warehouse) or `dbt-fabricspark` (Lakehouse), T-SQL dialect.
- **Orchestration**: Fabric Data Pipelines or an external orchestrator.
- **Power BI**: a quick report on top of the data.
- **Cost**: capacity is billed even when you don't use it, so keep an eye on it.
